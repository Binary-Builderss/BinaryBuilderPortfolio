Salesforce development and automation

We customize Salesforce, write business logic, automate workflows, connect it to other systems and look after existing orgs. We work directly with companies, and as extra development hands for Salesforce consulting partners.

What we build

  • Apex classes and triggers for logic that declarative tools can't handle.
  • Salesforce Flow automation for approvals, record updates and multi-step processes.
  • Lightning Web Components for custom UI in Lightning pages and apps.
  • REST API integrations between Salesforce and the other systems you run.
  • Troubleshooting and performance work on existing implementations: governor limits, slow queries, automations that keep failing.

Who it's for

Companies that run Salesforce and want custom development or automation, and Salesforce consultancies looking for specialist developers on a well-defined piece of work. We take on both direct projects and subcontracting.

How we work

We start with the business process, agree on realistic goals and deliverables, and write tested Apex your team can maintain. If your org already does something well, we build on it instead of rebuilding it.

Other services

  • Backend development and API integrations
  • Custom software and web applications
  • Software maintenance and technical problem solving
