About BinaryBuilders

We're an independent software studio run by two developers. Our background is in enterprise software, backend systems, frontend applications and business process automation.

Why we started

We started BinaryBuilders to help companies solve real technical problems with software that's reliable, easy to maintain and ready to grow. We'd rather keep things practical and clearly defined than add complexity nobody needs.

What we bring together

Enterprise engineering, modern web development, systems integration and business automation. In practice: Java and Spring Boot backends, React and Next.js applications, Salesforce development, and the integrations that tie them together.

How we work

  • Problem first We make sure we understand the problem before proposing a solution.
  • Realistic scope We agree on requirements and deliverables we can actually meet.
  • Maintainable code Clean, tested and easy for your team to take over.
  • Reuse before rebuild If an existing system does the job, we connect to it.
  • Clear communication From start to finish, whether we're building something new or working in an existing codebase.

Small on purpose

We're two developers, not a big consultancy.

You talk directly to the people writing the code, it's always clear who owns what, and the work stays focused. We do our best work on well-defined projects, integrations, targeted development tasks and ongoing maintenance, either directly with companies or as subcontractors for agencies and consultancies.
